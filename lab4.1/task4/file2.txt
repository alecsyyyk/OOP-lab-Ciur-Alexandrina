Student life has changed a lot over time. In the past, 
only rich families could send their children to school. 
Students studied mostly religion, history, and mathematics.
 Later, schools became open to everyone, and more subjects 
 were added. University life grew more active, with clubs, 
 sports, and cultural events. In the 20th century, technology 
 started to help students study faster. Today, students use laptops
  and the Internet for research and online classes. Many study abroad 
  to experience different cultures. Student life now means not only learning,
   but also growing as a person and preparing for the future.
