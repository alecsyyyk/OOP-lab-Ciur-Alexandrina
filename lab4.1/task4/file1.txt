Long ago, people used nature freely without thinking about its limits. Forests were cut down, rivers were polluted, and many animals disappeared. 
In the 19th century, people started to understand that nature needed protection. 
The first national parks were created to save forests and wildlife. Later, organizations like Greenpeace and WWF were founded to fight for the planet.
 Schools began to teach students about recycling and saving energy. 
Today, many countries have laws to protect the environment.
 People plant trees, clean beaches, and try to live in a greener way. Nature protection has become one of the most important global goals.
